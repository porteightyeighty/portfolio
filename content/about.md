# About

I'm Matt, a software engineer in London.

I studied English and Comparative Literature at Leeds, took web programming modules alongside it,
and kept building things after I graduated. In 2021 I joined Impact Box, a Salesforce
consultancy for charities, and in 2022 I co-founded Impact Box Co-operative with the rest of the
team. Over five years as a CRM consultant and developer I delivered more than 30 Salesforce
projects, usually from gathering requirements through to the data model, the Apex code, testing
and handover. Most of the interesting work was engineering: Apex services written test-first,
integrations with Stripe, payment processors and other REST APIs, and data migrations from
systems like Airtable.

Most of what I knew about software design was self-taught, so in 2024 I started a part-time MSc in
Computer Science at Birkbeck alongside work. I finished it in 2026 with a Distinction. My project
was [rust2lctrs](/rust2lctrs), a translator from Rust into input for a termination prover, and
during it I contributed a change to the prover itself.

## What I'm looking for

Software engineering roles, on Salesforce or off it: building backend services, integrations and
the systems around them, on a team that cares about code quality. After five years shipping work
for clients at a small co-operative, I'd like to work on larger systems with a bigger engineering
team.

I'm based in London and can work on-site or hybrid.
